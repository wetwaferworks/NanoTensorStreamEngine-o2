Verdict: every VQ variant leaves E essentially unreconstructed
(0.96 to 0.99) and barely moves the global number, because E's
error is already priced into the pack at 0.99. PQ d8 K256 is the
best VQ-family result (global 0.5816 for about 0.56 MB). The fp16
carve-out is the best fidelity decision: 4.19 MB against a
75.15 MB artifact (+5.6 percent) removes 39.8 percent of the
global squared error outright (0.6922 to 0.5371), and fp16 is
indistinguishable from fp32 here (0.000208) at half the bytes.
Recommendation for format v3: E ships as fp16 raw; PQ d8 is the
fallback if bytes ever outrank fidelity.
